Our Vision & Mission

Meet the Coaches

RallyPoint Tennis is built on the belief that premium sports instruction should be accessible to everyone. Our founders established this camp strictly as a non-profit community effort to encourage physical activity, build confidence, and teach strategic match management to local tennis enthusiasts. Both directors bring intense enthusiasm and years of court competition to ensure a safe, technical, and high-energy environment for every player.

Camp Options

Rise & Rally or Sunset Smash

Choose between two perfectly structured daily formats that suit your schedule and playing style. Both programs include technical feedback, tactical scenarios, and conditioning.

7:00 AM - 8:30 AM

Morning Rise & Rally

Beat the heat and start the day strong

Focuses heavily on crisp stroke development, agility footwork circuits, and cardiovascular conditioning. Perfect for players who want to build consistent rallying, develop deep baselines, and master high-percentage shots before the sun is at its peak.

  • Warmup & technical demo
  • Stroke consistency and consistency drills
  • High-intensity feeding drills
  • Served & return placement workshops
Duration

90 Minutes

Focus

Technique & Agility

5:30 PM - 7:00 PM

Evening Sunset Smash

Matchplay, tactics, and social rallies under the lights

Emphasizes tactical positioning, pressure play, match management, and dynamic competitive games under court floodlights. Ranging from doubles strategy drills to tiebreak tourneys, this session is perfect for putting training directly into competitive action.

  • Tactical pattern of play workshops
  • High-intensity match-situation drills
  • Supervised point play & coaching feedback
  • Social tiebreak mixer tournaments
Duration

90 Minutes

Focus

Tactics & Matchplay
